Build a Keyboard Pyramid +1 is an arcade obby where you scoop up colorful keycaps, stack them high on your back and build your way to the top of the pyramid!

Features

  • Grab Keys in the Pit: Scoop up colorful keycaps and stack them high on your back. Watch out for jackpot spacebar and Enter keys worth a whole pile!
  • Build the Pyramid: Climb up and drop your keys into the glowing slots. Every key placed pays cash, every layer brings you closer to the top!
  • Train at the Gym: Run on treadmills for Speed and lift on bench presses for Strength to carry bigger loads. Higher-tier gym pads multiply your gains!
  • Upgrade Everything: Grab more keys at once, drop them faster, reach further and earn bonus cash with powerful upgrades.
  • Five Sweet Pyramids: Finish a pyramid to rank up and celebrate, then build the next one in Chocolate, Candy, Mint, Gold or rainbow RGB!
  • Style Your Builder: Unlock skins, trails and emotes, then climb the leaderboards to show who's the top builder!

Controls

Desktop

  • WASD / Arrow Keys = Movement
  • Mouse = Look around
  • Space = Jump
  • Mouse Wheel = Zoom in/out

Mobile

  • Joystick = Movement
  • Touch Screen = Look around
  • Jump Button = Jump
  • Zoom Buttons = Zoom in/out
